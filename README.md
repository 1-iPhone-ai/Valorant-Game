# Valorant-Game

A single-file browser FPS built on Three.js r128. Open the latest `(V1.x) valorant-fps.html` in a browser to play.

Earlier versions are kept as separate files.

## Controls

- **Keyboard and mouse:** press **P** in game for settings and keybinds.
- **Controller (PS4 / PS5 / Xbox):** connect it and press any button. The full layout is in **Settings → Controls → Controller**.

## Regression tests

The `tests/` folder holds headless browser tests. They need no GPU, because Chromium renders WebGL in software. They check:

- **Load:** the game loads without errors.
- **Shader compiles:** none happen after warm-up, including when switching quality.
- **Abilities:** every agent's abilities and ultimate cast, and ground effects stay circular.
- **Ability numbers (V1.9.1):** the Duelists' gameplay numbers live in one table, `AGENT_TUNING[agent][slot]` (C, Q, E and X, each with a `look`); it keeps that shape, holds only numbers, and nothing changes it during play.
- **Maps and modes:** all 7 maps × 3 atmosphere modes.
- **Controller:** input works through a simulated gamepad.
- **Game modes:** Wave, Retake and the 30-bot Range play through planting, defusing, detonation, round changes and the range scorecard.
- **HUD and settings:** the HUD has no overlapping panels at common window sizes, and settings survive a reload (and the opt-out sticks).
- **HUD layout (V1.9):** one bottom row of ability slots and the ammo, within 96 px of the bottom edge at 1280×720; ability names show under the icons by default (Settings → HUD → Ability Names, also on for saves from before the setting; a saved choice is kept), every name of all 29 agents fits its slot (a long one on a smaller line or two lines), and hiding them gives 56 px slots; nothing permanent in the middle of the screen (20–85% of the height, between the side columns) and every weapon name fits, at HUD Size 80–130% and 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 1024×600; the agent strip shows on an agent change or a new round and is gone after 4 s, with the passive and the [G]/[TAB] hint in the controls help; the weapons are a list at the right edge above the score; teammate plates are one line (the agent name) over a health bar, at most 96 px wide, at 35% opacity near the crosshair; the FPS readout is one line ("144 FPS / Medium": the quality name cycles the quality, the FPS opens Settings) and the detailed mode keeps its quality pill; the FPS graph is off for new players and a saved choice is kept. Settings → HUD → HUD Layout → CLASSIC puts the V1.8 HUD back (checked against V1.8 to within 2 px at all three window sizes: the centre stack with the agent bar, the weapon row, the three FPS readouts and the old teammate tags) and V1.9 comes back after.
- **Audio and hit feedback:** 3D enemy audio and hit feedback work.
- **Viewmodel animation:** animated draw, a recoil spring that settles, reload with the magazine out and back in, inspect, and shell casings that land and clear; the gun always returns exactly to rest.
- **Weather and time of day:** weather is off by default; Map weather gives each of the 7 maps its own (rain, storm, snow, sand, leaves); rain wets the floor and the floor dries when it stops; storms flash lightning (softened by Reduce Flashes) and fade back exactly; Low turns it off; Sunset and Dawn move the sun and refit the shadows, and Daylight restores them exactly; no shader compiles on any map.
- **Reflections and lit abilities:** the floor reflection runs every frame on Ultra and Cinematic, every other frame on High only while the floor is wet, and never on Medium or Low; the mirrored camera clips everything below the floor; ability blasts, fire zones and gunfire light the floor and walls, fade out exactly, and the 6 nearest are used; Low turns them off; Cinematic draws light shafts from them; no new shader programs.
- **Kill cam and highlights:** after a death the kill cam replays the last seconds from the killer's side, names the killer, puts every bot and the camera back exactly after each replay frame, holds the respawn until it ends, and Space skips it; the setting turns it off; in Match mode the round MVP card shows, the best play of the round replays and the next round waits for it, and the result screen shows the round strip, K/D, MVPs and the best play.
- **Main menu and agent select:** the start screen pauses the game under an orbiting camera with an agent preview; keyboard, mouse and controller drive it (Start pauses, D-pad and A select, B backs out); agent select describes all four abilities of all 29 agents and LOCK IN starts the chosen mode; Esc opens the pause menu and the round timer stands still; Skip Menu On Start is remembered; the menu adds no shader programs. (The other tests start in play: the harness skips the start screen unless a test asks for it.)
- **Breakable world:** glass panes and explosive barrels sit on open floor on every map (away from spawns and bot cover) with no shader compiles; bullets go through a wood crate but not the same thickness of metal, leaving holes on both sides; shot or enemy-fired glass shatters and stops blocking, then comes back; barrels explode, hurt bots and you nearby and set each other off; a new round restores everything and the Breakable Props setting removes them.
- **Your own body:** shown in play in your agent's colour, standing under and just behind the eye, and hidden in menus, replays, while down and with Show Body off; legs swing in opposite directions when walking, settle when standing, tuck in the air, and the body squats when crouched; it casts a shadow; no new shader programs.
- **Deathmatch and Spike Rush:** Deathmatch (F8) is a free-for-all board where your kills and deaths count, bots kill each other, everyone respawns quickly at a random spot, first to 40 wins and the result screen shows your placing, and the extra bots leave with the mode; Spike Rush (F9) is first to 4 with no buying, the same random gun for everyone each round, full abilities, four power-up orbs (speed, armour, ult points, health) and fresh orbs each round; both are in the main menu.
- **Sound redesign:** each gun has its own layered sound (the suppressed Phantom has no crack) and bursts fire three times; footsteps follow the surface (concrete floor, wood, metal and barrel tops) and the weather (wet in rain, crunchy in snow); crouched steps are shorter and quieter; landings thud; the echo is stronger in an enclosed space than in the open.
- **Cast animations:** casting raises the off-hand into view with energy in the agent's colour and throws it (mirrored when left-handed); ultimates gather with orbiting wisps and release with a small shake; a cast never interrupts a reload; the knife slashes alternate sides and the heavy attack lunges; the gun always returns exactly to rest, with no new shader programs.
- **Ability effects and camera shake:** ability bursts, ground rings, smokes and fire zones emit sparks, glow, smoke and embers that expire, all drawn by shaders compiled in the warm-up and switched off on Low; nearby blasts shake the view and it returns exactly, with Camera Shake off by setting and with Reduce Flashes.
- **Cinematic quality:** the level above Ultra is never picked by Auto; its post-processing (AO, bloom, sun shafts, grading, FXAA) compiles in the warm-up, adds no shader programs when switched on, keeps the overall exposure, and turns on both High Detail settings.
- **Match mode and death recap:** buy-phase lock, round wins and losses with economy, no respawn mid-round, plant/detonate and bot plant/defuse, halftime swap, bots routing to a site on their own, victory screen, and the death recap in Match and Wave modes.
- **Music, ambience and HUD size:** music and ambience are off by default, play per map when switched on and fade out when switched off; HUD Size 80–130% scales every panel with no overlaps at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 1024×600.
- **Accessibility and bot skill:** Reduce Flashes softens washes and flashbangs and stops the low-health pulse; the cyan colour-blind option recolours bots; bot tactics scale with Bot Skill (Iron never flanks, Radiant peeks pre-aimed).
- **Bot tactics:** every map has cover spots; hurt bots take cover out of sight, peek to shoot and never fire while running; pairs flank, and a bot whose partner dies takes cover.
- **Skyline (the 7th map):** a neon rooftop city at night, in the F4 cycle after Split and in the main menu's MAP button; the city around it shows only there; Map weather rains; it has its own music; both spike sites and the spawns are clear; you can climb the stairs onto a skybridge, walk across it and under it, and step up onto the side decks and the helipad; antenna lights blink; no new shader programs.
- **Movement:** crouching while sprinting slides you low and faster than a sprint, ends on its own, has a cooldown, carries its speed into a jump and tilts the view (back level exactly), and never starts from a walk; jumping into a ledge up to chest height (the 2.2 m Skyline deck) climbs onto it, but not a 3 m block or without moving into it; in the air momentum carries further than on the ground, you can steer, and you never gain speed; the Slide and Mantle settings turn them off and are saved.
- **Progression:** kills give 100 XP (+50 for a headshot) and wingman kills none; round wins, plants, defuses and finished matches give XP; levels show a toast and unlock player cards, sprays and four new gun skins (locked skins are refused in Settings until earned, the original skins stay free); three daily challenges depend only on the date, keep their progress all day, change the next day and pay their XP when done (real slides count); the main menu Career screen shows level, challenges and every reward, equips what you own and refuses what you don't; [N] sprays on the wall in front of you with a short cooldown, cleared on a map change; progress survives a reload; no new shader programs.
- **Skins and the workshop:** a skin colours the body, trim and glowing parts separately; the main menu WORKSHOP turns your gun in place of the figure as you pick body, trim, glow and tracer colours, a finish and glow strength (the preview shares the real gun's materials), BACK without saving puts the equipped skin back, SAVE & EQUIP stores a named design in one of three slots that then shows in Settings and the Career screen, and it is still equipped after a reload; no new shader programs.
- **Photo mode:** J (or PHOTO MODE in the pause menu, never from the start menu) freezes the game, round and spike timers included, and hides the HUD, gun and body; the free camera flies with WASD and Q/E, looks by dragging and zooms with the wheel while the player stays put; filter, brightness and letterbox show on screen and in the photo, which Enter saves as a full-resolution PNG; J or Esc puts the view direction, field of view, brightness and gun back exactly and the game runs again; no new shader programs.
- **Aim trainer:** a main menu mode with no bots or wingmen and a drill picker (letting go of the mouse for it does not pause the game); Gridshot keeps three targets up, real shots hit them, time the reaction and score at least 100 each, a miss costs 25, hit targets come back elsewhere and ammo never runs out; Flick shows one smaller target; Tracking scores time with the crosshair on a moving target and stops when you look away; the result card shows accuracy and average reaction, saves a new best (and not a weaker run) and awards XP; leaving brings the bots and wingmen back; no new shader programs.
- **Ranked:** RANKED in the main menu runs a full Match; casual rounds and the Range no longer move your rank; five placements against Gold bots, then a rank between Bronze 1 and Platinum 3; a bigger win or a better game gives more RR and a close or well-played loss costs less; 100 RR promotes with a one-match demotion shield, dropping below 0 demotes, Iron 1 stops at 0 and Radiant keeps counting; bots play at your level and your Bot Skill setting comes back after; leaving after a round counts as a loss; the rank is saved.
- **Ranked screens:** the main menu RANKED screen shows placement dots while unranked, then your rank, RR, peak, record, bot level and last matches, and PLAY RANKED goes through agent select into a ranked match; on the result card the RR bar starts at your old RR and moves to the new one; promotion and demotion (in red) banners; your rank shows under your card and on the HUD.
- **Bot colours and animation:** bots are drawn as soldiers (helmet and visor, arms holding the rifle, chest rig, knee pads and boots) in their archetype colours, as are the wingmen, the menu preview and the kill-cam ghost; they walk with bending knees, flinch, recoil and topple over when killed; the drawn body stays inside the original hitboxes, which stay fixed.
- **High detail characters and guns:** the setting is off by default; when on, bots, wingmen, guns and hands are drawn with rounded, detailed shapes while hitboxes, weapon skins and shaders stay unchanged.
- **High detail maps:** the setting is off by default; when on, it adds textures and props on all 7 maps without new shader compiles or a brightness shift, and the Low profile turns it off.
- **Gunplay (V1.8):** every automatic gun has its own spray pattern (8–12 bullets, then a bounded left-right sway), two uncompensated sprays draw the same shape whichever way you face, first shots standing still are near-perfect and each gun resets on its own time; aim error follows your real speed (accurate below 30% of run speed: a running Vandal at 20 m mostly misses, SMGs and shotguns barely suffer, pistols sit between, crouching still helps) and the Recoil Trainer and its pill show it; damage falls off by range (none for the Vandal, three bands for the Phantom, steep for SMGs, pistols and shotguns); the Ghost, Bandit, Stinger, Spectre, Bucky, Guardian, Ares and Odin have stats, viewmodel parts, their own sound, a kill-feed name and skins with no new shader programs; Match and Ranked use one primary + one sidearm + knife bought on a credit ladder (sell back what you bought this phase, keep your guns when you survive, lose them when you die, a Classic each half), and after losing the pistol round a rifle and heavy armour are out of reach; every other mode picks any gun for free from the armory [B] or [V]; Spike Rush stays locked to the round's gun (overclocks included). Held ADS bursts start fresh, a gun taken out starts a new spray, the first shot after a reset leaves exactly from the crosshair, infinite-ammo sprays keep swaying, a cancelled reload never refills later, High Detail hides the detail parts a new gun replaces, and muzzle flashes sit at the barrel end.
- **Ascent rebuilt (V1.8):** attackers spawn west, defenders east, A site north and B site south; three lanes (A Main, Mid, B Main) run between solid buildings 8–10 m tall with no line of sight from one lane into another (even from the raised spot on a site), each with a 3.5 m chokepoint under an arch; both sites are enclosed 16 × 16 m rooms with three entrances, cover, a thin wallbangable wall and a platform up a flight of steps; mid is a courtyard with a fountain and a link to each site; the longest sightline is about 45 m; attackers reach either site 1–2 s after defenders; the map has 150–250 pieces through the existing batching. Sites, spawns, callout zones, bot spawns, respawn spots and ult orbs now come from each map's own data, and the six other maps are checked against V1.7 (pieces, sites, spawns, ult orbs, callouts) so they play exactly as before. Bots that cannot see you now go round walls by route (A* on the map's walk grid) instead of walking into them, as do flanks, runs to cover and the wingmen; attacking bots run from their spawn to either site without sticking; all 8 modes start on Ascent with the player and bots on open floor; a bot pushed into the corner where a site's platform meets its stairs is stepped out instead of staying wedged inside.
- **Ultra Realism (V1.8):** a graphics tier above Cinematic, chosen by hand only (Auto never steps into it, and Auto from it drops to High), with its own ULTRA REALISM box in Settings → Video: a master switch and one switch per feature (sky and fog, materials, Ascent detail, lighting, post processing, reflections, guns and hands, characters, effects). The checks cover: no shader compiles after the warm-up when switching to it or toggling any feature; going back to Cinematic restores every material, geometry, light, pass and the same picture; hitboxes, map collision and the bots' walk grid are identical on every tier; the switch itself does no heavy work (surface textures, the light bake and the reflection probe are prepared in small steps or in a worker); without WebGL 2 the tier falls back to Cinematic and says so; the FPS readout names the tier and shows the frame time (in its tooltip since V1.9); and Ascent detail appears only on Ascent, with the light rebaked for each map and time of day.
